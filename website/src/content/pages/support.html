Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things people ask most. Don’t see yours? Email us above.

Does TidyByte upload my photos? 01

No. TidyByte has no server that receives photos. iPhone, iPad, and Mac library cleanup runs locally on your device. In iOS builds with Google Photos enabled, the app connects directly to Google for sign-in, storage quota, and the items you select. On Mac Direct builds, external network requests only happen if you opt into an AI provider in Settings or check Sparkle updates.

What about iCloud-only photos? 02

TidyByte works with your full library on iOS and Mac, including assets stored only in iCloud. On iPhone and iPad, the system downloads them as needed or lets you swipe past; on Mac, TidyByte identifies iCloud-only items and can download full-resolution copies before reviewing or compressing.

Are deleted photos gone immediately? 03

No. Deleted photos on iPhone, iPad, and Mac move to the system Recently Deleted album for ~30 days before permanent removal. On Mac, cleaned files normally move to Trash (and non-deleting APFS clone folding or archive restores are recorded in SQLite undo history), though asking external package managers or simulator tools to purge caches cannot be undone by TidyByte.

Will it lower the quality of my photos? 04

No cleanup tool re-encodes behind your back. Only the explicit Photo Compression, Video Compression, or Live Photos-to-still tools re-encode media, and you choose the settings — including Keep Both vs. Replace & Trash Original (reversible via Trash or Recently Deleted), HEIC/HEVC options, Max File Size targets, and Check Result Sizes on Mac.

How are duplicates detected? 05

Exact duplicates are matched by SHA-256 hash of the file data. Near-duplicates use Apple Vision feature prints to find visually similar shots.

Is there a free trial or subscription? 06

TidyByte is free, with no in-app purchases and no paywalls. Every feature is available to every user.

Does TidyByte support iPad? 07

Yes. TidyByte is a universal app that runs natively on iPhone and iPad. Every cleanup tool works on both, the swipe interface adapts to the iPad’s larger screen, and iPad supports all orientations.

Does TidyByte work on iPhone Duo? 08

Yes. The same app runs on iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo, and the swipe interface adapts to the Duo’s outer and inner displays with nothing to configure. Every cleanup tool is built for both displays.

What is the difference between Direct and App Store Mac builds? 09

Direct Mac builds ship as a signed, notarized disk image in 21 languages with Sparkle auto-updates, the system-wide Monitor, the tidybyte CLI and tidybyte-mcp inside Tidybyte.app/Contents/MacOS/ (preview-first by default; execute and undo require confirm: true), and optional LaunchAgent APFS clone-folding. Sandboxed App Store Mac builds omit system-wide Monitor, Sparkle, CLI/MCP, external AI gateway calls, and LaunchAgent automation, focusing safely on user-selected folders and Photos.
